How client groups are assigned

Groups are mutually exclusive and assigned from the full selected-period ledger before the other filters, in this order: 01 net debt (invoices less credits) over $200,000 · 02 every invoice over 90 days · 03 a mix of invoices over and within 90 days · 04 every invoice under 14 days · 05 every invoice under 60 days · 06 every invoice 90 days or less · 07 everything else (credit-only or undated). The movement analysis applies the selected period's group membership to earlier periods.
