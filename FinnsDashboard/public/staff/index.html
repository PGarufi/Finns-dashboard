How every number is calculated

Billable staff

Every staff member in the Pay Categories export is measured, at every office. A person is listed in the dashboard only where receipts or invoices are recorded against them somewhere in the selected financial year; anyone with no billings recorded all year (administration, support and other non-fee-earning roles) is calculated but not displayed, so the table stays a list of fee earners. Because the test looks across the whole financial year, a fee earner stays in the table for a quiet month rather than disappearing and reappearing.

Totals, subtotals and targets are built from the rows on screen, so the firm and location figures cover the fee earners listed and not the salaries of staff with no billings.

Locations

Offices are reported as one place. The payroll entity prefix is dropped, so FIN - Sydney and SCS - Sydney are consolidated into Sydney — including for a person paid by both entities in the same office, whose pay, receipts and invoices combine into a single Sydney row.

Staff who change office

Each month is reported under the location the payroll recorded it at. Where a person moved between offices during the year they appear under each office, marked ‡, carrying only the months that office paid them — so pay, targets, receipts and invoices for those months sit with that office. Where a single month spans two locations, that month is apportioned between them in proportion to the pay recorded at each. Annualised salary and targets are calculated separately for each location from the months it paid, so the location figures add back to the person’s combined total.

The Staff detail tab always combines every location for the person selected and shows the split between offices.

Pay + super

For each month, Amount plus SG Super from every pay category row for that person, excluding any pay category whose name contains “Bonus”.

Estimated annual salary + super

Total pay + super across the financial year to date, divided by the number of loaded months in which that person was actually paid, multiplied by 12. Counting only paid months means a starter, leaver or someone on unpaid leave is annualised at their true rate rather than being diluted by months they were not employed.

Targets

Annual receipted target and annual invoiced target are each the target multiple (default 3.5) × estimated annual salary + super. A period target is the annual target × the months in the period in which the person was paid ÷ 12 — so a single month is one twelfth, a full quarter one quarter, and year to date scales with the months actually loaded.

Receipted and invoiced

The staff-level Contribution total from the Staff Receipted Funds and Staff Invoiced Funds reports. Contribution is the share of each invoice or receipt attributed to that fee earner, so staff totals add to the firm total without double counting.

Traffic lights

Green at or above the green threshold of the period target, amber at or above the amber threshold, otherwise red. Both thresholds are adjustable in the control bar.

The two annual-target columns are an exception: they display year-to-date receipts or invoices as a percentage of the full-year target, but the traffic light grades them against the pro-rata target for the months paid so far (annual target × months paid ÷ 12). A fee earner tracking exactly to target therefore shows green from the first month, even though the annual percentage is still small. Hover a cell to see the pro-rata percentage behind the colour.

Where the data lives

Uploaded reports are held in a single shared store behind the sign-in, not in each person's browser. Anyone signed in sees the same periods, and an upload by one person immediately becomes the firm-wide view; open dashboards pick the change up within a minute and jump to the newest month. Removing a period removes it for everyone.

Things to keep in mind

  • Annualised salary is an estimate built from actual pay. A month containing five weekly pay runs, back pay or a leave cash-out will lift it; a part-month start or finish within a paid month will lower it. Check the Staff detail page for anyone whose annualised figure looks wrong before acting on their percentage.
  • Receipts and invoices are matched to payroll on the full name first, then on surname and a compatible given name — identical, a shortening such as Greg for Gregory, or an initial. A shared surname alone is never enough, so two unrelated people with the same surname are not merged. A payroll name contained within the reported one also matches, which covers middle or preferred names and a maiden name added after marriage. Every name matched on anything other than an exact spelling is listed on the Data page for checking, and any name that cannot be matched is listed there rather than silently dropped.
  • A fee earner is identified by having receipts or invoices recorded against them, not by their location code, so someone whose billings are all credited to a supervising partner will not appear. Rows marked with a dagger were paid in only some of the loaded months; rows marked with a double dagger were recorded at more than one location.
  • Recently started fee earners will always look red. Work in progress builds before it is invoiced, and invoices are receipted later again, so a full target is only a fair test once someone has been billing for a full cycle.
